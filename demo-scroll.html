App Installation View

Redesigning the data experience for developers installing apps on Wix

2025 · Developer tools · Wix
→

Developers installing apps on Wix had no visibility into what data the app would access. I redesigned the installation flow to surface permissions clearly and give developers confidence at the moment of installation.

What I Did

  • Mapped the full installation journey across first-time and re-installation flows
  • Designed a new permissions display that categorised data access by type
  • Created a layered disclosure model — summary on install, detail on demand
  • Ran usability tests with 6 external developers and iterated based on findings
  • Delivered specs for mobile, tablet, and desktop breakpoints

Impact

  • Developers reported significantly higher confidence in installation decisions
  • Support tickets related to unexpected data access dropped markedly post-launch
  • The permissions pattern was adopted as a standard across other install surfaces

Bottom Line
